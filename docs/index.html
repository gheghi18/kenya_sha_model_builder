Build the computer that sets Kenya's health insurance bills

Kenya's health authority (SHA) doesn't know most people's income. So a computer model guesses it from a few questions about their home, family and belongings. Each household then pays 2.75% of the guess every month.

How is this worked out?

A linear regression on the chosen test households: "pays too much" (income guessed over 20% too high) against household characteristics, controlling for real income. The published methodology ran the same regression for "poor but called not poor". Only results with p < 0.05 and at least 30 households are shown unless you tick "show all". Categories are compared with a reference, e.g. Baringo for counties.
